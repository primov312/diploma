## Design Context

### Users
Everyday customers exploring demo catalogs, estimating affordable amounts, updating their account, and reviewing saved financing decisions. Partner catalogs demonstrate the product handoff into this same customer flow.

### Brand Personality
Formal, clear, dependable. Help customers feel oriented and confident without implying real lending, payments, affiliation, or unsupported service promises.

### Aesthetic Direction
Navy navigation and introduction panels with light content surfaces. Source Sans 3, aligned financial numbers, outline icons with short visible labels, compact spacing, restrained corners and borders. Full viewport width with 16px mobile and 24px desktop edge padding. Avoid pink, turquoise, lavender, gradients, decorative shadows, and long marketing copy.

### Design Principles
1. Put the customer's next action and financial information first.
2. Use consistent navigation, colors, icons, and typography across account pages and catalogs.
3. Keep form labels, validation and decision reasons visible; disclose methodology and technical metadata in expandable details.
4. Adapt complete navigation and forms for phones, tablets, keyboard use and zoom.
5. Identify illustrative examples and synthetic data next to relevant figures; preserve existing working flows.
